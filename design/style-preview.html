What changes from the old style

  • No thick ink outlines. Shapes are soft solid blocks; depth comes from 3D renders and shadows.
  • 3D objects (flame, coin, gift, trophy and one per category) will be AI renders in one consistent style. The ones above are hand-coded stand-ins.
  • Sticker buddy appears at key moments only: Today's Challenge, best answers, celebrations and empty states.
  • [Bracket] words in lavender highlight one word in a headline, as in Klubi.
  • Real photos on challenge cards and customer avatars (stock photos from Unsplash or Pexels).
  • App name "shiftly" is a placeholder until we pick the name.
